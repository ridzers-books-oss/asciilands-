Controls

WASD move · Mouse aim · Click/Space swing · Shift sprint
Q/E rotate camera · Wheel or +/- zoom
F search, open doors, interact · I backpack · 1-8 use items · J fish log · H help
Touch: left stick moves, buttons act.

Scavenging

Search fridges and cupboards with F, open your backpack with I. Press F next to a dead zombie to loot it. Dirt mounds hide hoe heads and hammer heads: dig them up with F.

Crafting

Use the crafting station. Grass makes string, string makes rope. Rod + rope = handle, then hoe head or hammer head + handle makes a tool. Rod + string = fishing rod.

Fishing

Craft a fishing rod, stand at the pond or ocean and press F. Wait for a bite, then press F again. J opens the fish log.

Farming

Equip the hoe and press G (or F on bare ground) to plow. Pick grass to find seeds, click a seed to select it, then press F on plowed soil to plant. Fill a bucket at the pond or ocean with F, then F on the plant to water it. Plants only grow while wet.

Graphics

For the best look, turn on hardware acceleration in your browser (Chrome/Edge: Settings > System > "Use graphics acceleration when available", then relaunch). Without it the game falls back to software rendering: no shadows, rougher visuals and lower frame rate.

Infection

Zombie bites infect you slowly. Medkits slow it. If you turn, a zombie version of you spawns where you fell and you respawn. Watch for fast runners and slow, brutal brutes.
